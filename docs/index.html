Privacy Policy for EarnMoney

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

EarnMoney ("we", "our", or "us") operates the EarnMoney Android mobile application (the "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how user data is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our Application.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and collect minimal data required to provide application services and advertising rewards:

  • Device Identifiers & Advertising ID: We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, which collects standard Advertising IDs (AAID) and device parameters to serve relevant advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
  • Network Information: The Application checks network connectivity (Wi-Fi or Cellular) to respect user preferences for Wi-Fi-only video playback.
  • Application Preferences: Your earned reward points, account balances, and app settings are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide video shorts, banner ads, and rewarded advertisements. Third-party ad partners may collect device information in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Data Disclosures

3. Data Protection & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. All network transactions use secure SSL/TLS encryption.

4. Children's Privacy

EarnMoney is intended for a general audience aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
